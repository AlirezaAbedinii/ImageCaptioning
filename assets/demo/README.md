# Demo results

Captions generated by the GAN-refined model on held-out COCO test images, with
their BLEU scores. These are the best-scoring examples reported in the thesis
(Chapter 4, "Model results": the "very good" group, BLEU > 0.8). They were
selected with `best_caps` in the notebook. A typical caption scores around
0.55–0.6.

![Result gallery](gallery.png)

| Image                                  | Generated caption                                     | BLEU  |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | :---: |
| ![](01_surfing.png)                    | a man is riding a surfboard in the ocean              | 0.963 |
| ![](02_bathroom.png)                   | a bathroom with a sink and a shower curtain           | 0.927 |
| ![](03_skateboard.png)                 | a man is riding a skateboard on a cement wall         | 0.922 |
| ![](04_motorcycles.png)                | a group of motorcycles parked on the side of a road   | 0.863 |
| ![](05_tennis.png)                     | a man is swinging a tennis racket at a ball           | 0.835 |
| ![](06_living_room.png)                | a living room with a couch chair and a chair          | 0.827 |
| ![](07_cat_sink.png)                   | a cat is sitting in a sink in a bathroom              | 0.824 |

**Average BLEU on the test set: ~0.57**, roughly a **10 % improvement** over the
plain encoder–decoder baseline from the adversarial / SCST refinement.

BLEU here is character-level (NLTK `sentence_bleu` applied to raw strings), so it
is not comparable to the word-level BLEU-4 in published papers. See
[`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`](../../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md#7-results).
